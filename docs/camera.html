📹 Octarine Camera Overlay

Draggable, resizable, shape-shifting webcam overlay for any webpage.

← Octarine Suite Standalone Whiteboard GitHub

1. Get the Bookmarklet

Drag the button below directly into your browser's Bookmarks Bar (press Ctrl+Shift+B or Cmd+Shift+B if your bookmarks bar is hidden):

📹 Octarine Cam
⬆️ Drag this button to your bookmarks bar ⬆️

2. Controls & Shortcuts

🖐️ Move & Reposition

Click and drag anywhere on the camera view to position it anywhere on screen.

📐 Smooth Resize

Drag the bottom-right circular handle, or simply scroll your mouse wheel over the camera.

🔄 Multiple Shapes

Click the shape icon or Shift+Click the camera to cycle Circle, Rounded Square, and 16:9 Wide.

🪞 Mirror Feed

Click the flip button in the overlay controls to mirror your camera horizontally.

📺 Presenter Fullscreen

Double-click the camera view or click the maximize button for near full-screen mode. Press Escape to return.

📹 Multi-Camera Switch

If you have multiple webcams (e.g. built-in and document camera), a switch camera button appears automatically.

3. Unobtrusive Recovery (When Camera is Turned Off)

When you click the × (Close) button on the overlay, the webcam stops completely (the webcam light turns off). To bring it back at any time:

  • Top-Left Corner Hover: Move your mouse cursor into the extreme top-left corner of your browser window. A floating camera button will smoothly appear—click it to turn the camera back on!
  • Keyboard Shortcut: Press Alt+C to toggle the camera on or off anytime.
  • Bookmarklet: Click the bookmark in your bookmarks bar again to toggle.
